Where the parser and your decisions disagree the same way on lines that look alike, the agreement is proposed here as a rule. Proposals come from each Act's last Teaching check. A rule changes nothing until you approve it; preview it first to see every line it would change across the Acts held here, and which of your examples it fixes or breaks. Approving changes the parser, so held Acts will ask to be re-parsed.
